# Refit at the community-mean intercept prior: what changes in the lessons

4 November 2026. Since 1b4a2cd, `runOccJSDM()` defaults to `b0_prior = "community"` for binary, occupancy and two-stage data with more than one species. Every shipped and lesson fit had been made with the fixed Normal(0, 1) prior. This note records the refit at the new default and lists every number or claim in the vignettes and README that changes, for Doug's review of Lessons 2 to 4. No lesson or Quickstart prose was edited; the `.md` renders of the Quickstart and Lessons 0, 2, 3 and 4 were regenerated. Later the same day Doug chose to keep the fixed prior for the alternative-prior arm only (see Decisions); this note describes that final state and keeps the community-prior version of that arm as a record.

## Summary

- **In the default-prior fits, almost nothing moves.** This community's true mean occupancy intercept is about -0.1 on the logit scale, where the fixed prior was already centred, so the community mean changes estimates by at most about half a percentage point. The pull towards the middle in Lesson 2 is unchanged (for example, PCR fit below 20%: 11.8 points too high before, 11.7 now).
- **mu_b0 converges in every default-prior fit at 3,000 + 6,000** (R-hat at most 1.0003, ESS at least 8,394), so the lesson burn-in was not lengthened.
- **The alternative-prior arm keeps the fixed prior (Doug, 4 November).** Fitted with the community mean, both alternative-prior fits (Beta(1, 4) contamination priors) had chain 4 in a mirror explanation for OTU_10: high occupancy (intercept 1.58, truth -0.68) with most detections blamed on field contamination (`theta0` 0.69, truth 0.08), while the other three chains agreed with each other (intercept -1.8, `theta0` 0.11). The longer fit had largest R-hat 9.24, 27 flagged parameters (2 before) and mu_b0 R-hat 1.28, and a burn-in of 15,000 did not free the chain. With the same seeds and `b0_prior = "fixed"`, no chain enters that mode, so both alternative fits were refitted that way: the longer fit now has 2 flags (OTU_6's collection intercept, R-hat 1.016, ESS 343, and its `theta0`, R-hat 1.014, ESS 385). This is one seed, so it shows the mode is reachable under the community mean, not that the community mean causes it. The lessons now compare a default fit with the community mean against an alternative fit with the fixed prior, so they should say so; suggested wording is under "Why the alternative arm uses the fixed prior" below.
- **The Quickstart's featured flag is now a covariate effect, not `theta0`.** In the refitted `quickstartresults` (same seed and settings) no `theta0` is flagged (largest R-hat 1.004, 1.039 before), which stopped the render at chunk `featured-flag`. Doug chose option (c): ship the refit and feature the flagged parameter with the highest R-hat. That is `beta_psi` for `protectedprotected` in Mammalia_Carnivora_3 (R-hat 1.015). Only code was changed; the sentences around it that no longer fit are listed below with suggested replacements, and the Quickstart renders against the installed package.
- **`computeSiteWAIC()` fails with its default quadrature orders on the new one-factor fit.** One posterior draw has a residual SD of 4.07, and the default ladder (15, 25, 41, 65) does not converge there. Lesson 4's displayed call (`computeSiteWAIC(fit_one_factor, threshold = 1)`, all draws) would stop with "Joint quadrature did not converge". The lesson's WAIC bundle was computed with orders 101 and 161 added (and 181 and 241 on the stricter check), which converge and agree with each other. Doug's session is fixing the package default on main.

## Why the alternative arm uses the fixed prior: sentences to add

No verifier checks the displayed fitting code against the saved fits, so these were left for Doug.

- Lesson 2, line 937, **prose**: "We refit the **same observations**, changing only the priors on laboratory and field-stage false-positive rates from Beta(1, 20), mean 4.8%, to Beta(1, 4), mean 20%". No longer "only". Suggested addition after that sentence: "This fit also keeps the earlier fixed prior on baseline occupancy (`b0_prior = "fixed"`) instead of the default community mean. With the community mean, one of its four chains settled in a mirror explanation for OTU_10, treating the species as common and most of its detections as field contamination, while the other three chains agreed on the truth-like explanation. Keeping the fixed prior here isolates the effect of the contamination priors.
