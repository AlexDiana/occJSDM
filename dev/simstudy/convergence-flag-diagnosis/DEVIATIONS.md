## Controller rulings

Pre-launch means made before any diagnostic fit started (the 40 fits were launched at 14:21 on 30 September 2026 and finished at 14:57); post-result means made after the Task 3 verdicts were known. Each line gives the decision and the reason recorded with it.

- R1 (pre-launch, Task 1): Task 1 may source only function definitions from `current-main-recheck/verify.R` (parsed and md5-checked) or reimplement the occupancy reconstruction; either way test (d), that the reconstructed probabilities average to the stored `psi_output` within 1e-10, is the proof. Reason: `verify.R` is a command-line script, not a set of functions to source.
- R2 (pre-launch, Task 1): `chain_separation()` also returns, for each key and species with any separated quantity, a chain grouping that splits the chains at the largest gap in that species' `mean_psi_original_sites` chain means; Task 2 uses these groups. Reason: Task 2 consumes chain groups that Task 1 did not define.
- R3 (pre-launch, Task 3): each variant runs 8 single-chain processes at the extended schedule, with the seeds of the first 8 extended-run chains. Reason: comparable to the extended run at modest cost. The ledger states the schedule as 10,000 burn-in and 40,000 retained; under R11 that became 40,000 post-burn iterations thinned to 10,000 retained draws.
- R4 (pre-launch, Task 1): `chain_anatomy(fit_paths, input_path)` accepts one or more fit files and concatenates their chains in the given order. Reason: needed for the 16 single-chain runs.
- R5 (pre-launch): no baseline package test run. Reason: the plan changes no production code, and `main` at `707540a` (a TODO-only change after `86c1199`) passed 967/0/1 on 30 September.
- R6 (pre-launch, Task 1): the latent loadings `L1` and `L2` and the site scores are reported but not used alone to label a species separated; the occupancy-scale quantity (`mean_psi_original_sites`) and the identifiable parameters decide. Reason: loadings can carry sign or rotation ambiguity and look separated with no change in occupancy.
- R7 (pre-launch, Task 1): "original sites" are the pr11 scorer's original 100 sites, as in the current-code comparison's scoring.
- R8 (pre-launch, Task 3): Task 3 is split into 3a (frozen protocol, runner, launcher, `modes.R` with tests, library install, equivalence check and plumbing pilots, with no diagnostic fits), then review, then 3b (the controller launches the frozen runs detached), then 3c (the frozen decision rules applied), then review. Reason: the pattern of the intercept-prior study, so that compute starts only after the protocol and runner pass review.
- R9 (pre-launch, Task 3): the diagnostic chains use distinct seeds, recorded per chain, rather than the saved input random-number state; the equivalence check still uses the saved state against the pr11 library. Reason: 16 independent chains are needed. The ledger recorded the cost if wrong as none because the chains are exchangeable; REPORT.md now treats that exchangeability as doubtful when comparing these chains with the pr11 chains.
- R10 (pre-launch, Task 3): variant (b)'s fixed value is species 6's generating `theta0` read from the input (about 0.038); the clone changes only that species' `theta0` update, and its function body is hashed and archived.
- R11 (pre-launch, Task 3): the extended and variant chains use `nthin = 4`: 10,000 burn-in and 40,000 post-burn iterations, keeping 10,000 retained draws per chain, not 40,000. Reason: 14 GiB of disk were free and unthinned files would need about 10 GB; the decision rules are unaffected at 10,000 draws per chain, at the cost of slightly noisier effective sample sizes. Confirmed by Doug.
- R12 (pre-launch, withdrawn): R12 first superseded R11, reverting to 40,000 retained draws with `nthin = 1`, because Doug had freed disk and `df` showed about 173 GB. It was withdrawn because `diskutil` showed 14.1 GB unallocated in the boot container (the larger figure probably includes purgeable space such as Time Machine local snapshots). R11 stands, as Doug confirmed.
- R13 (pre-launch, AMENDMENT-1): variant (c) isolates the split if, for each of the other nine species, the absolute change in its occupancy mean is below 0.01 or within 2 Monte Carlo standard errors of the difference (the two runs' standard errors combined); both are reported. Reason: the 0.01 limit alone can fail on sampling noise (species 7's standard error was about 0.019).
- R14 (pre-launch): a species-6 quantity agrees if its Task 1 label is `agrees` and its all-chain rank-normalised Rhat is at most 1.05. Reason: the implementer's reading, accepted because it matches the studies' threshold.
- R15 (pre-launch, AMENDMENT-1): a verdict "one mode only", naming the mode, when no chain of the extended run visits the other region (a visit being at least 1% of a chain's draws), instead of Mixed, with the exact shares reported. Reason: the plan did not cover this case.
- R16 (pre-launch): the change of mode method made before the freeze, tested only on the already-known saved pr11 fit and disclosed in README.md, is accepted. Reason: no new outcome had been seen.
- R17 (pre-launch, AMENDMENT-1): the primary mode assignment is anchored: each draw is classified by a frozen two-component classifier calibrated on the saved pr11 fit (near-truth from chains 1 and 3, mirror from chains 2 and 4, equal weights), using only quantities free in every variant (species 6's two environmental slopes, collection intercept and `mean_psi_original_sites`); `theta0` and `B0` are reported but not used, since `theta0` is fixed in variant (b) and squeezed by the prior in (a). Secondary checks are the `theta0` cut at the pr11 valley (0.135), where `theta0` is free, and the refitted mixture, with disagreements reported; the mode named under R15 is the anchored component; regression tests use pr11-shaped pseudo-chains split 1 and 15, 2 and 14, 14 and 2, 15 and 1 of 16, and 7 and 1 of 8. Reason: review found that the refitted mixture misassigns unbalanced splits, and a `theta0`-only rule would misread variant (b). The ruling named five pseudo-chain splits; the committed tests and `imbalance-check.csv` cover seven, adding 8 and 8 of 16 and 1 and 7 of 8 (AMENDMENT-1.md line 19).
- R18 (pre-launch, AMENDMENT-1): a chain with more than 5% of its draws far from both anchored components is in an unknown region, and the extended-run pattern then becomes other (verdict Mixed, with a note naming the chain); each chain's far-from-both share goes into `mode-mass.csv`; the anchored assignment feeds `mode-mass.csv` and the 16-chain figure; the pr11 far-from-both share (0.36% to 0.94% per chain) is recorded as the reference. Reason: it closes a silent-assignment gap found in re-review, where a probe chain 68% far from both components was still classed 100% near-truth.

Reason: the frozen protocol answered its questions, and a new experiment is Doug's call; the cost is that the cause of the pr11 entry stays open. The ledger's wording that the mirror is "rarely entered from the default start" was later replaced in REPORT.md by the chain counts and the layouts they come from (final review).

## Deviations from the plan

- Retained draws: PLAN.md's Task 3 budget has 40,000 retained draws per chain. The fits kept 10,000 retained draws from 40,000 post-burn iterations (`nthin = 4`, R11), because of disk space, as Doug confirmed.
- Task 3 was split into 3a, 3b and 3c (R8).
- The variants' schedule, which the plan did not specify, is the extended schedule with 8 chains each and the seeds of extended-run chains 1 to 8 (R3).
- The diagnostic chains use distinct seeds rather than the saved input random-number state (R9).
- The decision rules of PLAN.md were amended before launch by AMENDMENT-1.md, as README.md provides. It holds rulings R13 to R18: R13, R15, R17 and R18 change or add rules, R14 confirms a reading of `agrees`, and R16 accepts the pre-freeze change of mode method.
