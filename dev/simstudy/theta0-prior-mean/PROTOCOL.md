## Data and posterior sensitivity

Read all 60 selected ordinary nonspatial fits in the previous targeted interval study's manifest without modifying them: ten communities in each low/high laboratory-contamination condition under baseline (100 sites, two field samples, six PCR replicates per primer), four-field-sample and 300-site designs. There are ten species and two primers; the selected archives use production revision 2a75bf1. Known-U controls are excluded. Low-contamination cases are the main evidence. High-contamination cases are a deliberate mismatch to the contamination priors; poor performance alone is not evidence of a model defect. Preserve the known mirror labelling and all diagnostic flags.

Estimate sensitivity using full-joint posterior importance reweighting. For each retained joint draw, sum the Normal(1,variance 2)/Normal(0,variance 2) log density ratios over every collection slope and species. Do not weight species separately or alter the collection intercept. Use ordinary normalized weights for estimates and weighted equal-tailed 95% quantiles; record Pareto-smoothed importance diagnostics, ordinary weight effective sample size and the retained weight fraction by chain. An optional theta0 diagnostic uses the joint Beta(1,30)/Beta(1,20) ratio across all species. Compute source Rhat and bulk/tail ESS for theta0, collection coefficients, p and q. Source warnings, nonfinite diagnostics, Rhat above 1.05 or either ESS below 400 are flags. Importance Pareto k above 0.7 or effective size below 400 is a separate flag. Reweighting does not repair a poorly sampled source posterior or establish missing-mode mass.

Report mean theta0 error, absolute error, 95% interval width, coverage and directional misses. Use communities as independent replicates, and calculate within-community changes before averaging across communities. Keep flagged fits in the primary descriptive summary, with a sensitivity restricted to unflagged sources and acceptable weights. Ten communities per cell cannot provide precise coverage calibration, and this study is not simulation-based calibration with truth drawn from the prior.
