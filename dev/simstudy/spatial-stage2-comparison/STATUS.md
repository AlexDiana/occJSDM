- Reason 1, the gllvm arm does not fit in memory at either study site count. gllvm's spatial variational fit with two exponentially correlated latent variables (the first comparison's adapter, with the full "unstructured" variational covariance) needs about 0.7 GB at 100 sites, 7.1 GB at 200 sites and more than 33 GB at 300 sites, at which point the watchdog killed it, and about 38 GB at 900 sites in the first micro-timing run. The machine has 32 GB. The first micro-timing run thrashed swap for over two hours because of the two 900-site gllvm fits. This does not depend on any ladder setting, so no ladder step can fix it.
- Reason 2, the other arms are far too slow for the 48-hour rule at 900 sites. Measured per-iteration cost on the micro schedule (one chain, 50 warm-up and 150 retained draws, 900 sites, 12 species) and the all-extension upper bound that the plan prices (4 chains, 7,000 iterations, times 4.43, plus diagnostics and prediction):
- env arm: 0.0075 s per iteration, about 0.95 hours per job, 38 worker-hours for its 40 jobs. Not a problem.
- Hmsc spatial2 with the Full method: 1.65 s per iteration, about 58.7 hours per job, 2,349 worker-hours for 40 jobs, which is 294 wall-hours at eight workers on this arm alone.
- gp arm at 300 support points (the cheapest ladder setting): 11.4 s per iteration (a two-point check gave 9.5 s per iteration plus about 2 s fixed), about 394 hours per job, 15,754 worker-hours for 40 jobs, which is 1,969 wall-hours on this arm alone. At 900 support points it can only be slower.
- Even with the 300-site jobs priced at zero, the 900-site jobs of the Hmsc spatial2 and gp arms alone project to far more than 48 wall-hours. The plan's ladder (900, 600, 450 or 300 support points; Hmsc Full or 300-knot GPP) therefore has no passing step.
- The ladder's GPP rung does not run: Hmsc with the GPP method and 200 or more k-means knots at 900 sites fails with "the leading minor of order 2 is not positive" (a Cholesky failure of the knot covariance), and runs only at 100 knots or fewer (about 0.5 s per iteration at 100 knots and 0.26 s at 50 knots in a rough 10-iteration check, still too slow).
- Not yet measured when this entry was written, still running as discarded micro fits: Hmsc spatial6 with Full, and gp at 900 support points. They can only add cost, since Hmsc spatial6 has three times the factors of spatial2 and gp cost rises with support points. They do not change the conclusion.

A direct probe of it with the same data needed 0.3 GB and about 9 s for 30 iterations at 300 sites, and 1.0 GB and about 88 s for 30 iterations at 900 sites, so it is memory-feasible where the unstructured form is not. Using it would change the gllvm arm's approximation and needs a spec amendment.
