# Lesson 4 extension: replicated sampling, difficult ecology and traits

Approved scope: Doug approved the design on 23 September 2026 and explicitly included curved responses. This is a teaching experiment, not a general package ranking or a beta-release accuracy gate. Existing Lesson 4 pilot results remain unchanged.

## Fixed experiment

Use ten independent communities, seeds 26093001 to 26093010. Within a replicate, pair all applicable scenarios using the same random inputs. Use nested 100- and 300-site training samples and the same 300 independent test sites. All observations are perfect binary occupancy states; no spatial or observation submodel is fitted. Freeze all simulation coefficients, fitting rules and seeds before scoring truth. Never redraw an inconvenient community.

Ecological scenarios use ten species, two measured gradients and two independent hidden site factors. The baseline varies ecological coefficients slightly between independent communities. Rare-species scenarios change only the first three intercepts to -4. Correlated-predictor scenarios change only the predictor correlation to 0.85. Curved scenarios add -0.9 times gradient 1 squared to five species' scores. Each curved dataset is fitted with both linear responses and a model including the squared gradient. All four packages receive identical input. There are 400 package/dataset/model combinations, excluding restarts.

The trait experiment separately uses nested communities of ten and thirty species at both site counts, ten replicates. Drought tolerance affects the two environmental slopes by +1 and -0.6; the second measured trait has zero generating effect. Independent species deviations remain, with SD 0.35 per slope. Compare models with and without measured traits in occJSDM, gllvm and Hmsc, giving another 240 combinations. This tests recovery of trait relationships and the predictive benefit of traits. gllvm retains its species random-slope structure in both arms, so adding traits does not also add this hierarchy. The inspected sjSDM fork has no corresponding trait-fitting interface and is not scored for trait recovery. Species and site factors remain distinct; optional occJSDM latent species-trait factors are disabled in both arms.

All simulations use a logit link, as in the original lesson. Hmsc fits probit. Compare probability predictions on their common scale. Exact generating coefficient recovery is scored only where links match; Hmsc's trait-coefficient panels must identify their probit units and must not place logit truth on that axis. Sign/zero evidence and probability predictions can still be reported, with the link mismatch stated. A balanced logit/probit general benchmark remains a later study.

## Information, comparisons and figures

Learn predictor centring and scaling from each training sample only; form the squared predictor before scaling. Keep truth and held-out responses in separate files that fitting/selection code never reads. Verify identifier order, factor counts, transformations, input hashes and trait standardisation. A larger community keeps the same ten species, site conditions and observations for the shared species.

The primary target is new-site probability averaged over the unknown hidden conditions. Integrate over hidden conditions in both estimates and truth. Score mean signed error and mean absolute error in percentage points, by independent community, species and true-probability band, plus Brier and log scores for held-out occurrences. Pair errors within each community when comparing site counts, response forms or supplied traits. Report every individual replicate and the average; ten replicates do not support precise false-positive or power estimates. Never use species-by-site cells as independent replicates for uncertainty in these comparisons.

Teaching figures will show the generating scenarios, individual replicate errors and paired changes, curved true/fitted response examples, and true/fitted trait relationships for ten versus thirty species. Show estimates and known truth together wherever their definitions match. A trait's true absence of effect and an uncertain estimate of a real effect are different findings. Keep fit failures and unresolved diagnostics visible, with counts and reasons; do not quietly omit them from average errors.

## Fitting budgets, declared before results

Reuse the frozen four-package environment from the original comparison without upgrades. Its occJSDM R/C++ source matches current main; the intervening commits changed teaching material. Reuse gllvm 2.0.15 VA, Hmsc 3.3-7, and sjSDM fork v0.2.1 with PyTorch CPU and weight decay 0.0001. Every model has two hidden site factors. Run independent jobs in separate processes with computational threads restricted to one. Save settings, versions, warnings, failures and elapsed time.

Bayesian fits start with four chains, 2,000 warm-up and 4,000 retained draws. One longer fit (8,000 warm-up and 16,000 retained draws) is permitted when reported identifiable coefficient, covariance or fixed-grid prediction diagnostics fail Rhat <= 1.01 or bulk/tail ESS >= 400. Trait effects are included in diagnostics. A failed longer attempt remains flagged. gllvm uses three residual-based starts, then three further starts if the best acceptable objective is not independently reproduced; require convergence and maximum gradient < 0.01. Compute standard errors for trait models and retain failures of that calculation.

sjSDM starts use 3,000 epochs at learning rate 0.002 and 2,000 integration draws, followed by 1,000 epochs at learning rate 0.0002 with a fresh optimiser, as in the resolved pilot. Use full batches matching the actual training size. Start with three independent initialisations and allow three more if no second acceptable endpoint is within 0.1 penalised training log-likelihood units and one percentage point on a fixed prediction grid of the best endpoint. Select using checked penalised training likelihood, never truth error. Check numerical integration by refinement; do not confuse a noisy Monte Carlo objective with a stable solution. Unresolved cases stay provisional, including when the fixed budget is exhausted. This bounded replication budget is not a claim that the global optimum has been found.
