# Spatial medians and rank diagnostics

Recorded 28 September 2026 during the initial fits, before inspecting any half-Cauchy field-recovery, occupancy-error or coverage comparison. Four initial half-Cauchy fits had been inspected only for convergence; all had rank Rhat above 1.05. The sampler, prior, input data, starting values, fitting budgets and saved draws remain unchanged. This amendment supersedes the posterior-mean spatial outcomes and mean-based spatial diagnostics in the frozen `PLAN.md`. The original plan and calculations are preserved, rather than silently relabelled.

## Why the spatial means are not valid targets

This binary experiment uses 100 distinct sites and 100 spatial supports. The uncentred spatial basis H has full row rank. In the saved pilot, all ten range-specific matrices have rank 100, with smallest singular values between 0.019 and 0.927. Write the complete spatial field as F = sigma H Z, stacking species if necessary; Z has a standard Gaussian distribution and sigma has the half-Cauchy prior. Let d contain the proper, finite-valued intercept and environmental terms and s = 2y - 1 contain the observed binary signs. For a fixed range, the likelihood integrated over Z and d tends, as sigma tends to infinity, to the Gaussian probability that every component of s H Z is positive. Full row rank makes that orthant probability strictly positive. Integrating over the finite range grid retains a strictly positive limit.

Consequently, the posterior density of sigma has a positive constant times sigma^-2 as its upper tail. Its posterior mean is infinite. For any nonzero linear field functional l, the integrated quantity E[|l(F)| likelihood | sigma] divided by sigma tends to the positive expectation of |l(H Z)| on the correct orthant. Its absolute posterior first moment therefore diverges too. This includes individual field entries, species averages, centred fields and the projection on the true pattern. The same homogeneous-scaling argument applies to field RMS. Each raw field entry's matching-observation-sign expectation is infinite; its opposite-sign part is finite because t logistic(d-t) is bounded by max(d,0) + 1/e and the other predictor components have finite absolute prior means. Centring does not restore integrable posterior means.

The posterior itself remains proper because all priors are proper and the Bernoulli likelihood is bounded. Medians and finite quantiles remain valid. Occupancy probabilities and the finite-grid range are bounded, so their posterior means remain valid as well. More draws cannot make an infinite posterior moment finite. A finite-chain average is still computable, but cannot be described as an estimate of a finite posterior spatial mean.

This is a direct argument for this shared-scale Gaussian spatial model. Related separation and posterior-moment problems for independent and multivariate Cauchy regression priors are described by [Ghosh, Li and Mitra](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07170); a Gaussian vector mixed by a half-Cauchy global scale is not exactly their multivariate Cauchy model. The argument above, rather than that theorem applied verbatim, justifies the amendment.

## Amended outcomes, applied to both priors

Use the pointwise posterior median field for both the inverse-gamma and half-Cauchy fits. Compute raw error from that median field. For centred error, correlation and slope, subtract each species' across-site mean from its vector of pointwise posterior medians, and centre the true field in the same way. This explicitly means centre after taking pointwise medians; it is not the median of centred draws. The RMS of this point-estimate field is called median-field RMS. Report the posterior amplitude median and its 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles, plus raw pointwise field interval containment. Keep posterior-mean occupancy probabilities, their bias/error/interval containment, and range summaries unchanged.

Use rank-normalized folded/split Rhat and bulk ESS, plus ESS for the median and the two reported interval endpoints, for spatial quantities. Record quantile MCSEs and chain-median differences. Apply Rhat <= 1.05 and ESS >= 100 screening thresholds to the amplitude and the original per-species field-average, field-RMS and projection traces. Also inspect the individual field entries supporting the new pointwise median/interval summaries under the same thresholds. An unavailable diagnostic fails. These are screens, not proof of convergence. The trace named `field_mean_s` is an across-site spatial average within each draw; only its posterior median and rank diagnostics are used in the amended analysis.

The rank and quantile methods do not require the original variable to have finite moments: see [Vehtari et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.08008) and the [posterior quantile-ESS documentation](https://mc-stan.org/posterior/reference/ess_quantile.html). Native moment-based convergence warnings and original mean ESS/MCSE calculations remain in the legacy archive; they do not establish spatial-mean convergence or failure. Other existing rank checks and bounded occupancy mean-ESS checks remain applicable. Record native warnings separately.

## Selection and decision

Keep every longer fit already triggered by the original rules. If the amended spatial diagnostics flag an additional initial fit, require the same single, paired longer schedule for both priors, reusing a saved longer control when available. Apply the same finite escalation to the separate four-start check. Do not extend sampling indefinitely or choose runs according to field recovery. Preserve initial and selected results for sensitivity inspection.

The amended two-stage extension gate retains the original numerical conditions but applies field-error/correlation conditions to the explicitly defined median fields. It requires improved mean raw and centred field RMSE, improved mean pooled centred correlation, centred RMSE improved in at least six of nine communities, no unresolved amended spatial diagnostic flags in either prior or the initialization check, and no deterioration greater than 0.01 in range MAE or occupancy MAE. Any unresolved reconstruction-audit diagnostic threshold crossing also blocks extension. Community replication and the three fixed range strata remain unchanged. The legacy mean-based extension gate is inapplicable and must not be used to authorize an extension.

All rescoring is versioned separately under `robust-v1`; original fits/results are immutable and fingerprinted. No fitting-code or prior change is made in response to this finding. The unbounded half-Cauchy option requires this mathematical limitation to be clearly documented before any release decision. The experiment does not authorize changing the default prior.
