# Compact binary spatial results

These are extracts from three independently simulated datasets, one at each of three spatial ranges. See the [plain-language report](../spatial-beta-recheck.md) for the design, findings and limitations. The raw fits remain outside the repository; the fitting and analysis scripts regenerate them.

- `main-strata.csv`: nine dataset-specific summaries for low, medium and high true probabilities. `mean` is estimated probability minus true probability, averaged within that group. Multiply probability errors and their standard errors by 100 to obtain percentage points. The `lower` and `upper` columns describe the posterior distribution within a dataset; they are not uncertainty intervals across independent datasets.
- `all-strata.csv`: the main groups, five narrower bins and very-low/very-high probability subsets. Labels beginning `rare_` refer to extreme site probabilities within common species. They do not mean rare species were tested.
- `three-dataset-summary.csv`: equal-weight averages across the three datasets. `between_dataset_se` is the standard deviation of the three errors divided by the square root of three. `combined_mcse` measures numerical uncertainty from the finite MCMC draws separately. Species labels match simulated column numbers across distinct datasets, not a repeatedly sampled biological species.
- `parameters.csv`: spatial range, spatial SD and draw-level field summaries. `posterior-mean-metrics.csv` instead scores the posterior mean field and probabilities against truth. The SD of a posterior mean field is a different quantity from the average SD of individual posterior draws.
- `settings-seeds.csv`: fitting settings and simulation seeds. `reconstruction.csv`: numerical differences between an independent reconstruction and saved package outputs.
- `input-analysis-hashes.csv` and `source-hashes.csv`: the saved inputs, analysis script and source fingerprints. Input paths are relative to the local validation-results directory; repository scripts use repository-relative paths. All retained source files matched their recorded hashes when checked. The loaded DLL's recorded hash is retained, but its temporary library no longer exists for a fresh comparison.

R-hat is rank-normalized; `ess_mean` and `mcse` describe estimation of a mean. Missing diagnostics on constant traces are deliberate. These results support the provisional average-error target in this informative design; they do not establish performance for rare species, the two-stage detection model, or every support-point count.
