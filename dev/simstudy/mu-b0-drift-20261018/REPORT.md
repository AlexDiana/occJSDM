# Which latent term lets the community's occupancy intercepts drift: report, 18 October 2026

**Finding: none of them.** In the three fits of the 100-species mixing check where `mu_b0` failed to converge, the shift between the two chains in every species' occupancy intercept goes straight into the occupancy linear predictor and the latent occupancy states. The covariate term cannot move (the covariates are centred), the latent factor term does not move at all, and the spatial term moves by 2 to 3% of the intercept shift, in the same direction. The chains differ instead in how many site-species pairs are occupied: 43 and 55 more of 10,000 in the second chain of rare replicates 3 and 4, 46 fewer in ordinary replicate 2. The direction in which all the intercepts shift together is identified only through the latent states z, and the sampler moves along it one flipped site at a time. The move that addresses this is a collapsed Metropolis shift of `mu_b0` and all the B0 together, with z integrated out of the first-stage likelihood site by site and species by species. An interweaving (ASIS) step for `mu_b0` would not, because the centred `mu_b0` draw is not the slow part, and neither would centring the spatial or factor term, because neither moves.

## What ran

The three fits (`base_rare_cm_s100` replicates 3 and 4, `base_cm_s100` replicate 2) were refitted with `refit.R` on 18 October 2026 from 13:08 to 13:30, three workers with one RcppParallel and one BLAS thread each (`VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=1`), R 4.6.1 with Accelerate's `libBLAS.dylib`, mains power with Low Power Mode off, from a clean tree at cc94c98 (`main-provenance.txt`); 1,174 to 1,271 s per fit. Each chain ran the main run's 5,000 + 5,000 iterations with every 10th draw after burn-in stored (nburn = 5000, niter = 500, nthin = 10) and the latent states kept (`summarisedLatentPresences = FALSE`).

Two checks passed. Before the run, `check-storage.R` fitted the same dataset with nthin = 1 and the main run's storage, and with nthin = 10 and the latent states kept, at 50 + 100 iterations for the 10-species rare arm and 20 + 40 for the 100-species rare arm: every stored block and the chain seeds were identical at every 10th iteration, and the stored psi equalled logistic(eta) rebuilt from the stored terms to 4e-16 (`storage-check.csv`). After the run, the stored `mu_b0`, B0, collection intercept, theta0 and p draws of all three fits equalled the main run's draws at every 10th iteration exactly, 15 of 15 blocks (`reproduction.csv`), and the rebuilt eta matched the stored psi to 9e-16. So these are the main run's chains, and the terms below are the terms those chains had.

## The shift between the chains, term by term

Chain 2 minus chain 1 in the mean over sites, species and draws (`chain-difference.csv`), for ordinary replicate 2, then rare replicates 3 and 4:

-   `mu_b0`: -0.026, +0.165, +0.184. Mean B0: -0.025, +0.155, +0.181.
-   X B: 0 in every fit (the occupancy covariates are centred, so the term has no site-and-species mean to move).
-   Ks Bs, the spatial term: +0.0004, +0.0049, +0.0041 (the same sign as B0 in the rare fits: 3 and 2% of the shift, not an offset).
-   U L, the factor term: -0.0004, -0.0001, +0.0004. The column means of U differ by at most 0.003.
-   eta, the sum: -0.025, +0.160, +0.186. The whole intercept shift reaches the predictor.
-   Mean psi: -0.0041, +0.0043, +0.0057. Occupied site-species pairs: -46, +43, +55, from 4,821, 1,610 and 2,021 occupied in chain 1 (`occupied` is the draw-by-draw sum of z, so this is the same number as the psi difference times 10,000).
-   The detection side: mean collection intercept +0.017, -0.009, -0.006; mean logit theta0 +0.001, -0.028, -0.021; mean logit p 0.000, -0.004, -0.002. In ordinary replicate 2 the collection intercepts move with the intercepts (-0.68 of the B0 shift, in the opposite direction, the psi-theta trade-off); in the rare fits they barely move.

Species by species (`species.csv`, `species.png`): the regression slope of each species' shift in eta on its shift in B0 is 0.88, 0.96 and 0.90 (correlation 0.85, 0.95, 0.82); of its shift in the site-mean spatial term, -0.13, -0.04 and -0.10 (correlation -0.23, -0.11, -0.17); of its shift in the site-mean factor term, 0.004, -0.001 and 0.001. The sd across species of the B0 shift is 0.12, 0.11 and 0.13, and of the eta shift 0.12, 0.11 and 0.14. A term that compensated would have a slope near -1 and would leave the eta shifts near 0; the spatial term offsets a tenth of the shift at most, the factor term nothing. The B0 shift is uncorrelated with the true B0 (0.19, -0.13, 0.03), the true occupancy (0.18, -0.14, 0.03) and the number of sites with reads (0.05, -0.14, 0.11). Each species' shift in occupied sites follows its B0 shift at 13, 8 and 10 sites per unit (correlation 0.90, 0.76, 0.79). In ordinary replicate 2 the mean shift of -0.025 hides one species with true occupancy between 0.05 and 0.1 that shifts by -0.44 and three between 0.1 and 0.2 that shift by -0.14; the other 96 shift by -0.02 on average.

A site-level redistribution, a term moving up where psi is insensitive to eta and down where it is sensitive, would escape the site means. It does not happen either (`site-level.csv`). To first order the chain difference in mean psi is the mean over pairs of psi (1 - psi) times the pair's shift in eta: the B0 shift contributes -0.0036, +0.0124 and +0.0172 to it, the spatial term +0.00004, -0.0007 and -0.0024, the factor term 0. The spatial fields do differ site by site between the chains (within-species site sd of the shift 0.22 to 0.32), because the spatial range sits at a different grid point in each chain of every fit (0.074 or 0.107 in chain 1 against 0.010 to 0.014 in chain 2, each chain at its own point for all 5,000 iterations, the range mixing problem already on record), but that difference is almost uncorrelated with psi (1 - psi) (-0.002, -0.08, -0.13).

The spatial term also cannot absorb a constant cheaply (`basis.csv`). At the ranges the chains used, the least-squares fit of a constant over the sites by the spatial basis has R-squared 0.20 to 0.59, and the squared norm of the coefficients that shift it by one unit is 425 at range 0.107 and astronomically large at 0.01; with `sigma_bs` at 0.32 to 0.34, a shift of 0.17 would cost about 55 nats of prior per species. Even at the longest range of the grid (0.3, R-squared 0.96) it would cost about 3 nats per species.

## Within the chains

The same picture holds within each chain over the 500 stored draws (`partners.csv`, correlations averaged over the two chains). The mean B0 correlates with the mean eta at 0.997 to 0.999, with the mean psi at 0.98, 0.82 and 0.84, and with the occupied count at 0.96, 0.77 and 0.80; with the mean spatial term at -0.02 to -0.04, the mean factor term at -0.01 to -0.08, and the column means of U within plus or minus 0.07. Species by species, a species' B0 correlates with the site mean of its own spatial term at -0.02 to -0.03 and of its own factor term at -0.02 to -0.06, with its own occupied sites at 0.88, 0.73 and 0.74. On the detection side the mean B0 correlates with the mean collection intercept at -0.66, -0.38 and -0.49 and with mean logit theta0 at -0.44 to -0.46, the within-chain psi-theta trade-off, which between the chains only shows in ordinary replicate 2.

The mixing of the mean terms says the same (rank-normalised R-hat and bulk ESS of the 1,000 stored draws): `mu_b0` 1.06 and 39, 1.14 and 11, 1.18 and 8 (the main run's 10,000 draws gave 1.060 and 32, 1.128 and 13, 1.189 and 8); mean B0 1.10 and 19, 1.19 and 10, 1.27 and 6; mean eta the same as mean B0; mean psi 1.10 and 18, 1.05 and 82, 1.05 and 44; occupied 1.10 and 18, 1.05 and 75, 1.04 and 47; the mean factor term 1.00 and 790 to 990; the mean covariate term 1.00 and 400 to 960. The mean spatial term is itself badly mixed (1.17 and 76, 1.27 and 16, 1.85 and 25), through the range, but independently of B0. In the rare fits mean psi and the occupied count mix better than mean B0: the chains disagree about the occupancy itself by about half of its posterior sd (0.004 to 0.006 against 0.010 to 0.011), and by a full posterior sd on the logit scale, because for most pairs psi is small and flat in eta.

## Why the intercepts can shift together

Given z, each species' B0 is sharp: at the stored draws the Polya-Gamma weights sum to a conditional precision of 14 to 24 per species (sd 0.21 to 0.27, median 0.22 in the ordinary fit and 0.24 in the rare fits). Its posterior sd, with z integrated over by the chain, is 0.70, 0.79 and 0.80 (medians; 0.40 to 1.23 across species), 3.2 to 3.3 times larger. So the data fix each intercept only through z, and z is uncertain: in the rare fits 254 to 336 of the 4,461 to 4,487 pairs with no reads are occupied in a typical draw, 183 to 228 of the 2,242 to 2,398 pairs with reads in one PCR replicate, and 1,174 to 1,457 of the 3,115 to 3,297 pairs with reads in two or more (`pairs.csv`); the shift between the chains is spread over all three classes (rare replicate 3: +6, +6 and +31 pairs). The sampler moves an intercept only as its species' z change, a few sites per iteration, and moves `mu_b0` only as the intercepts' mean moves. The `mu_b0` draw given the B0 has sd 0.100 against a `mu_b0` posterior sd of 0.155, 0.191 and 0.195, so that step is not what is slow; what is slow is the common movement of all 100 intercepts through 10,000 latent states.

## Recommended sampler move

A collapsed Metropolis shift of `mu_b0` and all the B0, with z integrated out. Not implemented here.

-   Proposal: draw delta from Normal(0, tau^2); set every B0_s to B0_s + delta and `mu_b0` to `mu_b0` + delta. The deviations B0_s - `mu_b0` are unchanged, so the Normal(`mu_b0`, `sigma_b0`^2) prior terms of the B0 cancel and only the hyperprior Normal(0, `mu_b0_sd`^2) of `mu_b0` enters.
-   Acceptance: the product over sites and species of [psi' L1 + (1 - psi') L0] over [psi L1 + (1 - psi) L0], times the hyperprior ratio, where psi' is logistic(eta + delta), L1 is the probability of the site's M collection outcomes w given z = 1 (Bernoulli in theta) and L0 given z = 0 (Bernoulli in theta0). These are the per-pair quantities `sample_z_cpp_parallel()` already forms for the z draw, so the cost is O(n S) per proposal, and several proposals per iteration are affordable.
-   Placement: immediately before the z draw at the top of the iteration in `run_one_chain()` (R/chains.R), conditioning on w, theta, theta0 and the other terms of eta. The following z draw then comes from the full conditional at the new eta, which is what makes a step with z integrated out valid (a partially collapsed step followed by a fresh draw of the collapsed variable). The Polya-Gamma variables need no attention: `update_jSDMcoef()` draws Omega afresh from eta every iteration.
-   Scale: tau of 0.1 to 0.2, about `mu_b0`'s posterior sd at 100 species; adapt during burn-in if wanted. At 10 species the posterior sd is 0.45 to 0.55 and a larger tau applies.
-   What it buys: the common direction is then explored according to the z-integrated likelihood, the information the data actually hold about it, instead of one flipped site at a time.
-   Check: rerun `dev/simstudy/b0-community-s100-20261018/launch.sh main 5` with the move and apply that README's rule as it stands; the three fits here, with `check-storage.R`'s settings, show whether the shift is gone.

If ordinary replicate 2's slower species remain slow with that move, the next step is the detection side: there the collection intercepts shift with the intercepts, so a joint proposal that shifts all B0 up and all collection intercepts down along the within-chain ridge (correlation -0.4 to -0.7) would be the extension, with w integrated out as well. The rare fits give no reason to start there.

Ruled out by this diagnostic:

-   An interweaving or non-centred step for `mu_b0`: the `mu_b0` given B0 draw already moves half to two thirds of `mu_b0`'s posterior sd per iteration; the slow part is the B0 against z, which a reparameterisation of `mu_b0` does not touch.
-   A joint move of the intercepts against the spatial or factor term, or a centring constraint on either: neither term moves with the intercepts, between or within chains, in site means or site by site.
-   Longer chains alone: bulk ESS of 6 to 10 per 10,000 iterations means over 100,000 iterations for an ESS of 100, 3 to 6 hours per fit at 100 species.
-   A tighter `mu_b0` hyperprior: its sd of 2 is ten times the posterior sd; tightening it enough to matter would bias `mu_b0`, not speed it up.
