# Draft Indonesian teaching glossary

Status: proposed wording for review by an Indonesian scientist before classroom use or scored Indonesian model trials. Keep English terms alongside these translations where helpful. Function names, argument names, data columns and code remain unchanged.

| English | Proposed Indonesian wording | Teaching meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Occupancy | Okupansi | Whether a species occurs at a site; distinguish the probability from its realised presence. |
| Detection | Deteksi | Observing evidence of a species through the survey process. |
| Occupancy probability | Probabilitas okupansi | Probability that a species occurs at a site under the model. |
| Baseline occupancy probability | Probabilitas okupansi dasar | The package's intercept-based baseline summary; do not describe it as the probability for a specified site. |
| False negative | Negatif palsu | A present species is missed during field collection, PCR detection or both. |
| False positive | Positif palsu | A detection can arise from contamination in the field or laboratory. |
| Field sample | Sampel lapangan | A sample collected at a site; distinguish it from a PCR replicate. |
| PCR replicate | Replikasi PCR | A repeated PCR reaction on a field sample. |
| Environmental covariate | Kovariat lingkungan | A measured explanatory variable associated with the site environment. |
| Species trait | Sifat spesies | A species characteristic used to model differences among species. |
| Prior | Distribusi prior | A probability distribution specifying assumptions before conditioning on the data. |
| Posterior | Distribusi posterior | The distribution after conditioning on the observations under the model. |
| Credible interval | Interval kredibel | A posterior probability interval; distinguish it from a frequentist confidence interval. |
| MCMC chain | Rantai MCMC | One sequence of posterior sampling iterations. |
| Convergence | Konvergensi | Agreement and stability of sampling; diagnostic screens alone do not establish model validity. |
| Effective sample size | Ukuran sampel efektif | Estimated independent-draw equivalent for sampling precision; can exceed the retained-draw count and does not establish convergence. |
| Monte Carlo error | Galat Monte Carlo | Numerical error from finite correlated sampling, distinct from uncertainty in the posterior distribution. |
